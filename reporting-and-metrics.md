# Awareness Campaign Metrics

Key indicators include:

Open Rate

1. Measures visibility.

2. Click Rate

3. Measures interaction behaviour.

Report Rate

1. Measures awareness maturity.

Completion Rate

1. Measures training success.

---

# Example KPI Table

Metric | Purpose
------ | -------
Open Rate | Email engagement
Click Rate | Risk exposure
Report Rate | Security awareness level
Completion Rate | Training effectiveness
